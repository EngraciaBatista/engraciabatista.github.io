About Me

Where Data Meets Creativity

Hi there! I'm Engracia, an NLP Engineer and Data Scientist with a unique journey that bridges the arts and technology. Before diving into the world of data, I spent several years as a Project Manager and Business Analyst helping startups improve efficiency and launch successfully across various industries.

My journey into Business Intelligence began around 2016, combining three of my strengths: strategic thinking, logical and analytical reasoning, and artistic creativity (my first degree is in Fine Arts!). I later discovered Data Science and co-founded my own startup, Arrow Data Specialists.

Today I apply cutting-edge NLP and ML techniques at memoQ, working on production machine translation systems that serve millions of users, while pursuing my Software Engineering degree at Centennial College in Toronto.

I'm also deeply involved in the PMI (Project Management Institute) community, where I've mentored emerging data and project management professionals since 2017.
